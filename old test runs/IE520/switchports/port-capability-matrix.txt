IE520-28GSX port speed/duplex capability — MAC/Phy TLV campaign (AWPTCM-T955x / T9709)
tb470 IE520 stack (member 2 / swi_a) · 2026-09-03

Measured by CONFIG-ACCEPT on empty ports (no module = the PORT's own capability, independent
of any SFP) and on the two fitted copper SFPs.

  Config       | 1G SFP port (1-24) | SFP+ port (25/26) | AT-SP10TM(10G-T) | AT-SPTXc(1000-T)
  -------------+--------------------+-------------------+------------------+-----------------
  speed 10     |     rejected       |     rejected      |     rejected     |    rejected
  speed 100    |     ACCEPTED       |     rejected      |     rejected     |    ACCEPTED
  speed 1000   |     ACCEPTED       |     ACCEPTED      |     ACCEPTED     |    ACCEPTED
  speed 10000  |     rejected       |     ACCEPTED      |     rejected     |    rejected
  duplex half  |     rejected       |     rejected      |     rejected     |    rejected

Reject strings: "% <port>: Unsupported speed/duplex combination" · "% Duplex set failed" ·
"% Cannot set speed 1000 and half duplex".

Port-hardware conclusions (provable on empty ports, no module/link needed):
- NO front port supports 10 Mbit or half-duplex. Full-duplex; 100/1000/10000 only.
- 100 Mbit: 1G ports (1-24) only, and only with a 10/100/1000 copper SFP (AT-SPTXc). A 10G-T
  copper SFP (AT-SP10TM) runs 1000-only in a 1G port.
- 10000: SFP+ ports 25 & 26 only (both empty/available). Corrects an earlier note that no free
  10G port existed — 27/28 are the stackports, but 25/26 are open SFP+.
- 1000: every port.
Empty-port probing IS the way to prove port-level capability without a module (Terrence, 2026-09-03).
