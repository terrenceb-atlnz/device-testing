**Authentication batch (of the 9 screened, 7 not run):**
- (219) MAC Auth/Web Auth (T33375) and Tri-auth / VCS Master Failover (T38435) — empty bodies;
  T38435 also needs a working 2-member VCStack (u3 is off; u4/u5 are standalone).

  is not a platform capability.
- guest-vlan L3, IPv4/IPv6, hw-fwd enabled/disabled (T28126/7/8/9) — need traffic **between two
  supplicant ports** and/or **line rate**; one fabric host NIC + no line-rate generator.
